Proof of Hardin's width-six three-colour one-defect recurrence

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 4 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

We prove the empirical order-eighteen recurrence in R. H. Hardin's OEIS entry A229532, Formula field, revision 8 of 27 April 2021, for canonical three-colour n by 6 arrays with exactly one monochromatic horizontal, diagonal or antidiagonal bond, for every n >= 20. A 42-state transfer gives a finite integer certificate for all heights. The reduced rational generating function has minimal eventual order eighteen and discrepancy +864 at height nineteen. Seven smaller-strip recurrences and their sum-two variants follow from exact transfer identities. A general coefficient-extraction formula explains squared-denominator bounds.
